# Preliminary manuscript for academic review

This directory contains the current working version of the research manuscript, prepared for preliminary review and feedback by the authors' academic instructor.

**Manuscript title:** *A physics-informed neural differential equation for hourly urban ozone forecasting: A reduced-order dynamical approach*

## Available files

- **`Borrador_formato_final.docx`** — editable Word version.
- **`Borrador_formato_final.pdf`** — PDF version for convenient reading and review.

## Manuscript status

The manuscript is being prepared for potential submission to *Environmental Modelling & Software* (Elsevier). It has **not been submitted** to the journal and should be considered a preliminary review version, not the definitive submission file.

The documents may be revised, replaced, or removed as the authors incorporate feedback and complete the final checks.
